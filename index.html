01 — ABOUT ME

Turning research into useful evidence.

I work across environmental science and development research, with an academic background in Environmental Science and Disaster Management.

My interests centre on how communities respond to environmental change, how local institutions shape adaptation, and how research and data can support more sustainable decisions. I have contributed to academic and development projects involving field research, questionnaire design, data management, qualitative and quantitative analysis, and scientific reporting.
